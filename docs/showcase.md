# Built with any2orca

Projects that use any2orca, listed because the people who made them asked. Each line is in its maker's own words. Being listed means the project meets the rules below, not that I've reviewed or endorse it.

## Get your project listed

1. Post it in [Show and tell](https://github.com/KuzuriAo/any2orca/discussions/new?category=show-and-tell). The form asks for a name, a link, a sentence or two on what it does, and whether any file leaves the user's device.
2. Tick **List this project on the any2orca showcase** at the end of the form.
3. I add a line here with your description and link, and reply in your post when it's up.

You can also open a pull request that adds your line to the list below; answer the same questions in its description.

A post without the box ticked is just a post. Nothing is listed without your say-so.

## What a listing needs

- **You made it, or you speak for it.**
- **It says plainly whether a file leaves the user's device.** any2orca's own promise is that the file never does, and a listing here sits next to that promise. Your project can work differently (a print service has to receive files, for example), as long as it says so where its users will see it before they hand over a file. The listing says so too.
- **A link that works.**

Open source, free or paid are all fine. There is no paid placement, and the list is in the order projects were added.

## Coming off the list

Ask in your post, or open an issue, and the line comes off. I also take a line off if its link stops working, or if the project no longer says plainly what happens to users' files; I'll say so in your post first.
