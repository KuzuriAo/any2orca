Output-affecting: **Every filament in a converted file is 1.75 mm, the filament the U1 takes.** A Bambu H3C project keeps 2.85 mm for a filament on its 2.85 mm TPU PrintTool; left in the file, Snapmaker Orca computed the extrusion for filament nearly three times thicker, and the U1 would have badly under-extruded it. The filament's other settings stay the creator's, and a note names each filament that was changed.
