Output-affecting: **The Snapmaker filament option gives a Polymaker PLA the Polymaker preset Snapmaker Orca ships for it.** Snapmaker Orca installs three Polymaker presets for the U1's 0.4 mm nozzle (General, Silk and Tough PLA Family), and Snapmaker Space's written review rules name Polymaker next to Snapmaker and Generic as the filament brands it accepts. Until now `--filament-compliance snapmaker` named a Polymaker PLA Snapmaker PLA SnapSpeed. Now a filament that is Polymaker's, by the maker the project names for it or by a name such as PolyTerra, PolyLite, PolyMax or Panchroma, becomes the Polymaker preset for its kind of PLA, with that preset's settings: silk lines take Silk PLA Family, PolyMax PLA and the PLA Pro lines Tough PLA Family, the other PLAs General PLA Family. A Polymaker filament none of the three fits (PETG, ABS, ASA, a filled PLA, any filament on another nozzle size) follows the same Snapmaker-then-Generic order as before, and a filament already on one of these presets keeps it. Files converted with the Snapmaker option that hold a Polymaker PLA change; files converted with Original or Generic don't. With either filament option on, the notes also say when a plate has more than four colors, which Snapmaker Space's review turns down unless the print pauses at filament changes. Library: `resolveFilamentName()` takes the source's vendor (`{ vendor }`) and returns the `brand` it chose for; `matchFilamentBrand()`; `inspection.filaments[].vendor`. Profiles: `filamentNames.brands` in each release's `index.json`.
