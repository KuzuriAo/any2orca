Projects saved by ElegooSlicer and Flash Studio (Flashforge's slicer) are now named as supported sources, in the CLI's help and the package description. They were already detected and converted; every source slicer is now tested on the same project, which converts, slices in Snapmaker Orca, and uploads to Snapmaker Space with Snapmaker filament names.
